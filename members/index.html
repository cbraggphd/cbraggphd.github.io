For current lab members

The tools research assistants use in OPAL. Each one runs in the browser. The reflection log is the only one that sends anything, and it goes to the lab's own record.

Lab tools

Bookmark these on the phone or laptop you bring to the lab. Both open without a login.

  • After every session you run

    Lab reflection log

    Log each session the same day. Three short parts: what you did, how it went, and what you take from it. Speak or type; most logs take about three minutes. The entry goes to the lab log, you get a Log ID, and you can print a copy for your own records.

    Keep participants anonymous: no names, participant codes, or details that could identify someone.

    Not a log entry. A problem with a participant's safety, or a departure from the approved protocol, goes to Dr. Bragg directly, the same day.
    Open the reflection log
    opal.ccsu.edu/labrefl
  • XR Meditation Skill Transfer study

    OPAL Lab Tools

    Internal tools for the XR study, one per page. The VirtualSpeech score reader matches VirtualSpeech session reports to participants in the master sheet by date and headset station, then exports the VS1 and VS2 scores.

    It runs in the browser and uploads nothing. It fetches one library file when it opens, so it needs a connection for that.
