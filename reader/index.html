Listening in the car

On your iPhone:

  1. Open this page in Safari on the phone. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, so it opens like an app and keeps your recordings.
  2. Add a PDF from Files, email or OneDrive. Leave the screen on while it makes the recording.
  3. Press Play. It keeps playing with the screen off and through the car like any other audio.

From your laptop (quicker for long documents):

  1. Make the recording here, then press Save file. You get an MP3.
  2. Put the MP3 in OneDrive or iCloud Drive and open it in the Files app on the phone.

Recordings are kept in this browser on this device.
